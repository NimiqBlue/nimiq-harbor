What Harbor does

Harbor provides a self-custody interface to connect a compatible wallet, view supported assets and routes, request swaps and make on-chain payments where available. The Bank section connects you to an independent fiat provider; Harbor does not itself operate your bank account or guarantee a fiat transaction. Features, supported assets and availability may change or be restricted.

Connecting a wallet to Harbor does not itself require identity verification. Independent fiat providers may require identity checks or other information depending on the service, transaction, location or their policies. Their own terms, eligibility rules, fees and privacy practices apply.

Your wallet and transactions

You control your wallet and are responsible for protecting its keys and recovery phrase. Harbor will never ask for your recovery phrase or private key. Use only wallets and accounts you are authorized to use. Before confirming, check the network, asset and contract, destination, amount, fees, exchange rate, minimum received and any expiry shown by your wallet and the service.

Swaps and payments can involve real assets and irreversible transfers. Quotes can change or expire; transactions may fail, remain pending or execute at a different time than expected. Network fees, third-party fees, slippage, liquidity, token, smart-contract, wallet and infrastructure risks may result in loss. A gasless swap is not necessarily free: costs may be reflected in the quote or received amount.

Signatures, approvals and recovery

A permit, token approval or signed order can authorize spending. Review each request carefully. Depending on its terms, authority may remain valid after you close Harbor, disconnect, clear browser storage or your session expires. Those actions do not cancel a transaction or revoke an approval. Use the relevant wallet or on-chain tools to inspect or revoke approvals where supported.

An acknowledgement or provider status is not by itself proof of finalized on-chain settlement. If an outcome is uncertain, retain the operation details and check the exact transaction or order before trying again. Do not assume that a timeout means no transaction was submitted or that a second attempt is safe.
